Introducing Fika & Wine

Two cultures. One experience.

Inspired by the Swedish tradition of fika—a pause for coffee, something sweet, and conversation—Fika & Wine creates welcoming moments to recharge, unwind, and connect.

With Scandinavian simplicity and a Latin spirit of hospitality, we bring people together through thoughtful gatherings, conversation, and shared experiences.

Recharge. Unwind. Connect.

Our event philosophy

Pause with purpose.

From a refreshing break at work to an intimate social gathering, we believe the best connections happen when people have room to slow down and enjoy the moment.
